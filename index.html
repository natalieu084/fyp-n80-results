Exact、Asymptotic、Monte Carlo：p 值怎樣計？
N80!K88
Why use Monte Carlo 模擬?
N80!K89
精確檢定(Exact Test): 最完美做法是列舉出兩組數據所有可能的排序組合Permutations,但當樣本數稍大N >40,可能組合數會高達數百億種，電腦會直接卡死。 漸進近似(Asymptotic Test): 樣本數大時可以用常態分布估算,但如果樣本數太，估算出來的 p值會失真
N80!K91
Monte Carlo 模擬: 當 Exact 慢,Asymptotic 不準時，Monte Carlo 登場,它直接隨機打亂標籤模擬 10,000 次，用極快速度算出無限接近 Exact 的 p 值(並附帶99% 信心區間來告訴你這個p值有多準)

閱讀提示：Monte Carlo 用有限次模擬估計 exact p，CI 顯示模擬誤差。CI 水平與模擬次數以實際 SPSS output 為準；目前表格標示 95%。

1. GAIA — Generative AI Appropriation Scale

理論基礎： Adaptive Structuration Theory (AST) — 技術唔係你用唔用嘅問題，而係你點樣將佢融入你嘅工作流程。Appropriation = 你將工具「據為己有」嘅過程，唔係被動接受，而係主動改造。

量度嘅係咩： 你自己覺得自己點樣使用 GenAI — 係 self-report，唔係客觀能力。

5 個 dimension，由淺入深：

① Adoptive Appropriation（採納）
最基本層 — 你有冇開始用、用咗幾多。

  • 例如 item: "I have incorporated generative AI tools into my regular workflow"
  • 高分 = 已經 routine 咁用；低分 = 仲係偶爾試下

② Interface Appropriation（介面掌握）
你識唔識操作個工具本身 — prompt engineering、調 settings。

  • 例如 item: "I have learned to use different features of generative AI tools to get the results I want"
  • 重點：呢個係講操作技巧，唔係講你理唔理解 AI 點運作

③ Customised Appropriation（訂製化）
你有冇改造個工具去 fit 你自己嘅需要。

  • 例如: "I have customized my interactions with generative AI tools to suit my specific needs"
  • 高分 = 你唔係用 default，你會調整 prompt style、建立自己嘅 workflow

④ Integrative Appropriation（整合）
你有冇將 GenAI 同其他工具/流程結合。

  • 例如: "I have combined generative AI tools with other software or tools to enhance my work"
  • 呢個係最高階嘅「用」— 唔係孤立咁用 ChatGPT，而係將佢嵌入你成個工作系統

⑤ Ethical Appropriation（倫理使用）
你用嘅時候有冇考慮 ethical implications。

  • 例如: "I consider the ethical implications of using generative AI in my work"
  • 呢個 dimension 好特別 — 佢話你知 appropriation 唔止係「用得叻」，仲要「用得啱」

你 data 嘅含義：
GAIA overall α = .961，非常高。但 GAIA 同 GLAT 嘅 correlation 係 .032（即係零）。呢個正正 prove 咗：你覺得自己用得幾叻（self-report appropriation）同你實際知幾多（objective literacy）係兩回事。 一個人可以日日用 ChatGPT、覺得自己好 integrated，但其實唔明 GenAI 點運作。

2. GLAT — Generative AI Literacy Assessment Test

理論基礎： AI literacy framework，改編自 digital literacy 同 media literacy。

量度嘅係咩： 你實際上知唔知 GenAI 點運作 — 係 MCQ 考試，有對有錯，唔係問你覺得自己知幾多。

4 個 dimension：

① Know & Understand（知識層）
GenAI 嘅基本概念。

  • 例如 item: "What does 'generative' in generative AI refer to?" → 答案: The ability to create new content
  • 又例如: "What is a 'hallucination' in the context of generative AI?" → 答案: When AI generates plausible but incorrect information
  • 呢啲係 factual knowledge — 你知唔知個 term 乜意思

② Use & Apply（應用層）
你識唔識實際用 GenAI 去做嘢。

  • 例如: "What is the primary purpose of prompt engineering?" → 答案: To optimize AI outputs by carefully designing input prompts
  • 又例如: "Which of the following is the best strategy to improve the output of a generative AI tool?" → 答案: Provide specific, detailed prompts with clear instructions
  • 呢個 test 你唔止知概念，仲要知點應用

③ Evaluate & Create（評估創造層）
你能唔能夠 critically evaluate AI output、知道佢嘅 limitation。

  • 例如: "Which of the following is a limitation of current generative AI models?" → 答案: They may reproduce biases present in training data
  • 呢個係 higher-order — 你唔係 blindly accept AI output，你識得 judge 佢

④ Ethics（倫理層）
你理唔理解 AI 使用嘅 ethical implications。

  • 例如: "Which of the following is an ethical concern related to generative AI?" → 答案涉及 copyright, bias, misinformation 等

關鍵區別 — GLAT vs GAIA：
GLAT 係考試，有標準答案。GAIA 係問卷，問你自己覺得點。Jin et al. (2025) 做 external validity 嘅時候發現：GLAT 可以 predict AI-assisted task performance（β = .220, p = .040），但 self-report 嘅 ChatGPT Literacy Scale 完全 predict 唔到（β = -.159, p = .118）。 呢個就係點解你個 study 要用兩個 — 一個 self-report，一個 objective — 然後發現佢哋 r = .03。

你 data 嘅含義：GLAT 同 DTI 嘅 correlation（.440）係所有 pair 入面第二高。即係話：實際知識同 diagnostic thinking 有關係，但自覺用得叻就未必。 呢個對 PT education 好有 implication — 你要教 student 真正理解 AI，唔係淨係鼓勵佢哋用多啲。

3. DTI — Diagnostic Thinking Inventory

理論基礎： Bordage 嘅 knowledge-driven model of diagnostic reasoning。呢個 model reject 咗舊嘅 hypothetico-deductive model（即係「生成 hypothesis → test → confirm/reject」）。Bordage 話：重點唔係你跟咩 process，而係你個 knowledge 點樣 organized。

Knowledge organization 四個 level：

  1. Reduced（低）— 知識太少，無法做 diagnosis
  2. Dispersed（散亂）— 有知識但冇 organization，好多 irrelevant features 混埋一齊
  3. Elaborated（精細化）— 知識之間有好多 connections，用 causal reasoning 連結
  4. Compiled（壓縮）— Expert level，直接 pattern recognition，唔需要逐步推理

DTI 量度嘅係咩： 你做 clinical diagnosis 嗰陣嘅思維特徵 — 唔係問你識唔識 diagnose，而係問你 diagnose 嘅時候個思維過程係點。

用 semantic differential 格式 — 每個 item 係兩個極端之間嘅 6-point scale：

Flexibility in Thinking（思維靈活度）— 21 items
呢個 subscale 捉嘅係你 diagnostic process 有幾 flexible。

  • Item F4: 左端 "early in the assessment I tend to have one idea about the diagnosis" ←→ 右端 "early in the assessment I tend to think of a number of possibilities"
    • 高分 = 你一開始就 generate 多個 hypothesis；低分 = 你一開始就 anchor 咗一個
  • Item F5: "once I have an idea of the diagnosis, I am not easily swayed from it" ←→ "I find it quite easy to change my ideas about a diagnosis when faced with new data"
    • 高分 = 你識得 update；低分 = confirmation bias
  • Item F15: "in an assessment, I tend to focus on finding a single diagnosis" ←→ "in an assessment, I try to assess all problems presented by the patient"
    • 高分 = 你唔會 tunnel vision
  • Item F38: "I find it difficult to change my way of approaching a case" ←→ "I can easily modify my approach to individual cases as required"
    • 高分 = adaptive reasoning

Structure of Knowledge in Memory（知識結構）— 20 items
呢個 subscale 捉嘅係你嘅 clinical knowledge 點樣 organized。

  • Item S8: "I think of clinical findings as relatively isolated pieces of information" ←→ "clinical findings trigger thoughts in my mind about the underlying process"
    • 高分 = 你嘅知識互相連結，一個 finding 會 activate 相關嘅 pathophysiology；低分 = 你記住 facts 但冇 connect
  • Item S9: "knowledge I have about a particular disease tends to consist of a few key features" ←→ "knowledge I have about a particular disease contains many features, all of which are important"
    • 高分 = rich, elaborated knowledge networks
  • Item S13: "when I think of a particular diagnosis, the clinical features do not come to mind readily" ←→ "when I think of a particular diagnosis, I can readily think of many different clinical features"
    • 高分 = compiled knowledge — 你諗到個 diagnosis 就即刻知道 associated features
  • Item S33: "in my mind, different diagnoses are not clearly distinguishable from each other" ←→ "in my mind, different diagnoses are clearly distinguishable from each other"
    • 高分 = 你嘅 differential diagnosis 清晰；低分 = 好多 conditions 喺你腦入面 blur 埋一齊

4. RTQ — Reflective Thinking Questionnaire

理論基礎： Mezirow 嘅 Transformative Learning Theory。核心概念係 meaning perspectives — 你用咩框架去理解世界。Reflection 就係 examine 呢啲框架嘅過程。

RTQ 量度嘅係咩： 你學習嘅時候反思到咩程度。4 個 scale 代表 4 個 level，由低到高：

① Habitual Action（習慣性行動）— 4 items，非反思
學嘢嘅時候唔需要思考，自動化做。

  • "When I am working on some activities, I can do them without thinking about what I am doing"
  • "In this course, I do things so many times that I do them without thinking about it"
  • 高分 = 壞事。 代表你做嘢唔經大腦，冇 engage 到 learning。
  • 呢個係 Schön 講嘅 "knowing-in-action" — 但喺 learning context 入面，太多 habitual action 即係你冇 actively learn

② Understanding（理解）— 4 items，thoughtful 但唔算 reflective
你明白 content，但冇 apply 到自己身上。

  • "In this course, I have to understand the material taught by the teacher"
  • "To pass this course, I need to understand the content"
  • 呢個係 Bloom 嘅 comprehension level — 你 get 到個 concept，但冇 relate 返去自己嘅 clinical practice
  • 中間層。 係 necessary 但唔 sufficient for deep learning

③ Reflection（反思）— 4 items
你開始 examine 自己嘅 assumptions。

  • "I sometimes question the way others do something and try to think of a better way"
  • "I like to think over what I have been doing and consider alternative ways of doing it"
  • 呢個係 Dewey 講嘅 "active, persistent, and careful consideration of any belief"
  • 高分 = 你唔只 accept 知識，你會 question 佢，考慮有冇 alternative approaches

④ Critical Reflection（批判性反思）— 4 items，最高層
你質疑嘅唔止係 content/process，而係 why — 你嘅根本假設。

  • "As a result of this course, I have changed the way I look at myself"
  • "This course has challenged some of my firmly held ideas"
  • 呢個係 Mezirow 嘅 premise reflection — 你唔係問「我做得啱唔啱」，而係問「我點解會咁諗」
  • Transformative learning 發生嘅地方。 你嘅 meaning perspective 被改變

重要：HA 係反向嘅。
HA 高分 = 唔好（non-reflective）。U/R/CR 高分 = 好。所以你會見到 HA 同其他 subscale 嘅 correlation 方向唔同。

你 data 嘅含義：

  • GAIA → CR r = .669（最高 correlation pair！）：自覺 GenAI 用得越深入嘅 student，critical reflection 越高
  • GAIA → R r = .609：同 Reflection 都好強
  • GAIA → HA r = -.023（零）：Appropriation 同 habitual action 無關
  • GLAT → HA r = -.276（p = .031）：GenAI 知識越高，habitual action 越低 — 即係 literacy 高嘅 student 更唔會 autopilot
  • GLAT → CR r = .237（p = .035）：GLAT 同 CR 有關，但弱過 GAIA → CR 好多

呢個 pattern 好 make sense：

  • 自覺用得深入（GAIA）→ 同自覺反思多（RTQ-R/CR）correlation 強，因為兩個都係 self-report，而且 appropriation 本身就涉及 metacognition
  • 但客觀知識（GLAT）→ 同 habitual action 嘅反向關係更 clean — 你真正識嘅越多，你越唔會 mindlessly 做嘢

四個 construct 嘅關係 — 你個 study 嘅故事

用一個 clinical scenario 串埋：

一個 PT student 見到一個 knee pain patient。

  1. Habitual Action（RTQ-HA）低分 → 佢唔會自動跳去做 standard knee exam，佢停低諗
  2. Understanding（RTQ-U）→ 佢明白 anatomy 同 pathology
  3. Flexibility（DTI-F）高分 → 佢 generate 多個 hypothesis（meniscus? ACL? referred from hip?）
  4. Structure（DTI-S）高分 → 佢嘅知識 organized 到可以即刻 distinguish 呢幾個 DDx

而 GenAI 喺度扮演咩角色：

  • GAIA 高分 = 佢識得將 GenAI integrate 入呢個過程（例如用嚟 brainstorm DDx、challenge 自己嘅 reasoning）
